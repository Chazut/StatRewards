## StatRewards {.tabset}

### Overview

Survive 15 PMC raids and Prapor sends you a supply parcel. Die 20 times and even Therapist takes pity on you. **StatRewards turns the stats you build up in raids into repeatable rewards delivered through trader mail.**

There are **46 default milestones** covering combat, healing, looting, exploration and more. Send any message to **StatRewards** in your in-game friends list to see the 10 milestones you're closest to reaching.

### How rewards work

Each milestone has its own trader and loot pool. **Boss Hunter**, for example, earns you three draws from Jaeger's pool of weapons, gear, keys and roubles every three boss kills. **Varied parcels** is on by default, so a parcel mixes categories and avoids duplicate items while the pool has enough choices.

Want rarer payouts or different loot? Open **StatRewards** in SPT's web menu as an administrator. Adjust the rewards, preview a sample parcel, then save and restart the server.

### Installation

Extract the ZIP into your **SPT 4.1.x** game folder, next to `EscapeFromTarkov.exe`, and start the server. On an existing profile, tracking begins from your current stats. When updating, keep your existing `config/mod_config.jsonc` and `progress/` folder.
